## About Mobiliza

Mobiliza is an AI-powered church operations and member engagement platform designed to help churches understand, organize, mobilize and care for every member.

It brings the different arms of church operations into one intelligent workspace, connecting members, departments, cell groups, leaders, services, transportation, attendance, prayer activities and follow-up processes.

Rather than functioning as another church database, Mobiliza acts as a connected operations team that helps identify needs, coordinate responsibilities, automate routine processes and ensure that important activities never disappear unnoticed.

### Our Mission

To help churches become more connected, organized and intentional about caring for people through intelligent technology.

**See everyone. Know what's happening. Take action. Keep everyone connected.**
